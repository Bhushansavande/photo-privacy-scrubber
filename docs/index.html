Photo Privacy Scrubber

Photos carry hidden data: where you were, when, which phone, sometimes a serial number. Remove it before you share. Nothing is uploaded: it all happens on your device, and the picture is not re-compressed (no quality loss).

Options

What gets removed

  • GPS location, date and time, camera and phone model, lens, serial numbers
  • Editing history and author names (XMP), captions (IPTC), comments
  • Embedded thumbnails and data appended after the picture (some phones hide a video there)
  • PNG text notes (author, software, AI prompts)

Not covered: HEIC/AVIF photos (convert to JPEG first), videos, and anything visible in the picture itself (faces, street signs, reflections). Removing metadata does not make a photo anonymous.
